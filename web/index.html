Double-click to add a concept. Click a circle or line to activate it (Esc deactivates) and see its infobox; drag a circle to move it, empty space to pan; Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Cmd+drag to activate a group; wheel or pinch to zoom, Shift+wheel or Alt+wheel to pan. To relate circles, activate a circle and press +, then click another circle, or empty space to create a new connected circle and name it; Esc, right-click or the source circle cancels.
